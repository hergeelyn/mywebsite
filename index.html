About Me

The person behind the process

Keeping operations calm, organized, and moving forward

I'm Hergeelyn Rayos, an Office Staff and Business Operations professional based in Quezon City, with hands-on experience supporting executives, coordinating branch operations, and assisting customer-facing teams. My work centers on the details that keep a business running smoothly: scheduling, documentation, reporting, and the everyday coordination that turns a busy team into an efficient one.

I've supported leadership teams as an Executive Assistant, coordinated day-to-day activity as a Branch Coordinator, and built strong customer communication skills as a Customer Service Trainee. Across every role, I bring the same commitment — stay organized, stay adaptable, and make the people I support look good.

I thrive in fast-paced environments where priorities shift quickly, and I take pride in being the dependable point person who keeps workflows, calendars, and records in order — quietly, consistently, and with full confidentiality.
